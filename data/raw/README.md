## Datasets Used

### 1. Online Retail Dataset

Purpose: Used for historical retail transaction demand.

Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ulrikthygepedersen/online-retail-dataset


---

### 2. London Weather Data

Purpose: Used for external weather demand drivers.

Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/emmanuelfwerr/london-weather-data



---

### 3. UK National Holidays Dataset

Purpose: Used for UK bank holiday and calendar demand features.

Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivd24coder/uk-national-holidays-dataset


---

### 4. HR Analytics Dataset

Purpose: Used for employee demographics and HR attributes.

Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rishikeshkonapure/hr-analytics-prediction


---
